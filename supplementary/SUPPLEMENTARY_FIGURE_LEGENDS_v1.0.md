# Supplementary Figure legends

## Supplementary Figure S1. Sensitivity analysis excluding first TMDs

Sensitivity analysis of TMD-relative positional conservation after exclusion of all first TMDs. The histogram shows the distribution of median cross-species positional variances obtained from 1,000 within-protein synonymous-codon permutations after excluding TMD1. The observed median variance across 54 qualifying orthogroup–TMD units was 17.21 aa², whereas the median of the permutation distribution was 116.81 aa². None of the 1,000 permutations produced a median variance less than or equal to the observed value, corresponding to an empirical one-sided P value of 9.99 × 10⁻⁴.

## Supplementary Figure S2. Fixed-presence conditional positional null

Conditional positional-null analysis controlling for the observed cross-species presence pattern of low-adaptation segments. For each of 80 usable orthogroup–TMD units, the observed-positive species set was retained and null TMD-center-relative positions were sampled from species-specific conditional distributions generated from within-protein synonymous-codon permutations. The histogram shows the median cross-species positional variance across 1,000 conditional resampling replicates. The observed restricted median variance was 31.88 aa², compared with a conditional-null median of 93.20 aa². The empirical one-sided P value was 9.99 × 10⁻⁴.

## Supplementary Figure S3. Leave-one-orthogroup-out robustness analysis

Robustness of the TMD-start-relative conservation statistic to removal of individual orthogroups. Each point shows the change in the global median TMD-start-relative positional variance after removing one of the 76 orthogroups contributing to the primary analysis. The dashed horizontal line marks zero change relative to the full-data median of 22.70 aa². Leave-one-orthogroup-out medians ranged from 20.17 to 26.62 aa², with a maximum absolute change of 3.92 aa², indicating that the global result was not driven by any single orthogroup.

## Supplementary Figure S4. Positional quality control for matched soluble pseudo-anchors

Quality control of soluble-protein pseudo-anchor positions used in the matched membrane–soluble comparison. Absolute relative-position differences between membrane-family TMD targets and matched soluble-family pseudo-anchors are shown separately for TMD-start (A) and TMD-end (B) anchors. The dashed line marks the downstream quality-control threshold of 0.05. A total of 1,483 pseudo-anchors were constructed for each anchor type. Of these, 1,474 TMD-start pseudo-anchors and 1,478 TMD-end pseudo-anchors passed the 0.05 threshold. Median absolute relative-position differences were approximately 0.00042 for TMD-start and 0.00044 for TMD-end pseudo-anchors.

## Supplementary Figure S5. Family-level matched soluble–membrane variance differences

Family-level paired differences between matched membrane-protein and soluble-protein families. For each informative matched pair, the plotted value is the soluble-family median positional variance minus the corresponding membrane-family median positional variance. Positive values therefore indicate greater positional variance in the soluble family. Family pairs are ordered by the paired difference, and the y-axis is displayed on a symmetric logarithmic scale to accommodate both negative differences and large positive values. (A) TMD-start-relative comparison, with 12 of 17 pairs showing positive differences and a median paired difference of 140.36 aa². (B) TMD-end-relative comparison, with 10 of 17 pairs showing positive differences and a median paired difference of 75.00 aa². The corresponding family-level sign-flip and exact sign-test results are reported in the main text.
